## A season plan with one measured frontier adjustment

This controller starts from the public adaptive route published by
[tetsutani](https://www.kaggle.com/code/tetsutani/adaptive-farming-strategy-for-kaggriculture)
and independently published byte-for-byte by
[Roman Rozen](https://www.kaggle.com/code/romanrozen/strong-barnyard-economist). The base source is
SHA-256 `997e6bfc...f1b6`; attribution is part of the artifact, not an afterthought.

The route already combines a complete 720-turn season with three small closed-loop repairs:

- repair a blocked route when a weed appears;
- rank simultaneous sales by demand and price impact;
- pull a premium sale forward against a near clone, then repay the exact quantity on its original turn.

The only behavioral change here is deliberately narrow: premium-sale preemption searches
**four turns ahead before 3, 2, 1**. The frozen production route, clone gate, quantity cap, and
repayment bookkeeping are byte-identical to the public base. Longer is not automatically better:
the controlled sweep below falls apart at eight turns.

LIVE_CONFIG = {
    "episodeSteps": 720,
    "seed": 20260809,
    "townCenterSellInterval": 24,
    "farmHandCostMult": 1,
}
rows, representative = [], None
for side in (0, 1):
    agents = [str(MAIN_PATH), "starter"] if side == 0 else ["starter", str(MAIN_PATH)]
    env = make("kaggriculture", configuration=LIVE_CONFIG, debug=False)
    env.run(agents)
    final = env.steps[-1]
    assert len(env.steps) == 720
    assert final[side].status == "DONE" and final[1 - side].status == "DONE"
    rows.append({
        "seat": side,
        "candidate_bank": float(final[side].reward or 0),
        "starter_bank": float(final[1 - side].reward or 0),
        "margin": float(final[side].reward or 0) - float(final[1 - side].reward or 0),
        "steps": len(env.steps),
        "status": final[side].status,
    })
    if side == 0:
        representative = env
live = pd.DataFrame(rows)
assert (live.status == "DONE").all() and (live.margin > 0).all()
live.style.format({"candidate_bank": "{:,.0f}", "starter_bank": "{:,.0f}", "margin": "{:+,.0f}"})

## What the public games say about the old Baseline

## Evidence boundary

This notebook demonstrates source integrity, official-environment execution, and local controlled
crossplay. It does not claim an official Kaggle score, leaderboard rank, or universal win rate.
Ratings come only from completed competition evaluation, and public replays cannot reveal hidden
source code.

The controller is still anchored to a frozen season route. Weed repair, price-aware sell ordering,
clone detection, and exact repayment make it less brittle, but they do not turn it into a general
planner. The next useful improvement is conditional fourth-turn activation against very close
clones—not an unconditional extension beyond four, which the sweep already falsified.
